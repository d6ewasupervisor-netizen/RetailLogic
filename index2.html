1. Team Lead Procedures

Starting a Visit

Lead's Steps:

  1. Login to APK on the tablet
  2. Navigate to the scheduled visit
  3. Click the START button
  4. System automatically creates Home to Store travel entry with mileage
Note: Team members check in the same way they currently do. No changes to their check-in process.

Viewing Travel Information (Lead):

  1. Click 'ADD TIME PUNCH' button
  2. View the following information:
    • Travel Work Type (Home to Store, Store to Store, Store to Home)
    • Start and End Times
    • Miles traveled
    • Travel Time (total time traveling)
    • Paid Travel Time (compensated portion)

2. Merchandiser Procedures

Starting Work

  1. Get tablet from Team Lead
  2. Click 'ADD TIME PUNCH' button
  3. Enter your PIN
  4. Click SUBMIT
  5. Employee Time Punch window appears
  6. Click 'Start Work' button
  7. View your travel details (same as Lead view)
  8. Click 'Save & Close'
  9. Click YES to confirm
  10. Success message appears
⚠ IMPORTANT: If editing Start Work time creates overlap with travel time, a warning appears. Click YES to recalculate travel time automatically.

Ending Work

  1. Navigate to Shift Completion screen
  2. Click 'ADD TIME PUNCH' button
  3. Enter your PIN
  4. Click 'End Work' button

Adding Store to Home (REQUIRED FOR LAST VISIT)

⚠ CRITICAL: Always add Store to Home travel on your LAST visit of the day, even if travel is not paid. This prevents missed travel on future visits.
  1. After clicking End Work, 'Add Store to Home' button appears
  2. Click 'Add Store to Home' button
  3. Store to Home travel entry is added with mileage
  4. Click 'Save & Close'
  5. Click YES to confirm
  6. Success message appears

3. Editing In-Progress Visits

You can edit travel times and mileage during an active visit if errors occur.

Steps to Edit Travel Entry:

  1. Click 'ADD TIME PUNCH' button
  2. Enter your PIN
  3. Locate the travel entry you need to edit (Home to Store, Store to Store, or Store to Home)
  4. Click the pencil icon in the Manage column
  5. Adjust Start Time or End Time as needed
  6. Click the checkmark to confirm
  7. Select a Change Reason from dropdown (see Section 7)
  8. Add optional comments if needed
  9. Click SUBMIT
  10. Click 'Save & Close'
  11. Click YES to confirm
  12. Updated Travel Time and Paid Travel Time display
Note: The system automatically recalculates Travel Time and Paid Travel Time when you change start or end times.
↑ Back to Top

4. Deleting Travel Records

If a travel entry was added by mistake, you can delete it during an in-progress visit.

Steps to Delete:

  1. Click 'ADD TIME PUNCH' button
  2. Enter your PIN
  3. Locate the travel entry to delete
  4. Click the trash can icon in the Manage column
  5. Warning message appears: 'Are you sure you want to delete this time entry?'
  6. Click YES
  7. Select a Change Reason from dropdown (see Section 7)
  8. Add optional comments
  9. Click SUBMIT
  10. Click 'Save & Close'
  11. Travel Time and Paid Travel Time update to 0.0000

Re-Adding Deleted Travel:

If you deleted a travel record by mistake:

  1. Click 'ADD TIME PUNCH' button
  2. Click 'Add additional details' (blue + button)
  3. Select 'Travel to Store' from Work Type dropdown
  4. Click START
  5. Travel entry is recreated with mileage
  6. Click 'Save & Close' and confirm

5. Viewing & Editing Completed Visits

Team members can view and edit drive time and mileage after a visit is completed.

Viewing Completed Visit:

  1. Navigate to Schedule screen
  2. Locate the completed visit
  3. Click the 'View Time' button
  4. Employee Time Punch Overview window opens
  5. Review all travel entries with times, miles, and paid travel time

Editing Completed Visit:

  1. From Employee Time Punch Overview, locate the entry to edit
  2. Click the pencil icon
  3. Edit Start Time or End Time
  4. Click the checkmark
  5. Select Change Reason from dropdown (see Section 7)
  6. Click SUBMIT
  7. Click 'Save & Close'
  8. Click YES to confirm
  9. Success message appears
  10. Updated times display immediately
Note: Changes made in completed visits will sync with Time & Expense system. Supervisors can edit travel times without recompleting the entire visit.

6. Important Notes & Reminders

CRITICAL REMINDERS

  • ALWAYS add 'Store to Home' on your last visit of the day
    • Add it even if travel is not paid
    • This prevents missed travel on future visits
  • Travel between stores within 5 hours goes on the store you are going TO
    • Travel after work is always 'Store to Home'
  • When viewing completed visits, changes reflect Time & Expense updates
  • Supervisors can edit travel times without recompleting visits
  • Team Leads can now report exceptions on completed visits
  • A Change Reason is REQUIRED for all edits and deletions

7. Change Reason Codes

Required whenever editing or deleting travel entries. Select the reason that best describes why the change is needed.

Change Reason When to Use
Traffic Unexpected delays due to traffic congestion
Construction Road construction, detours, or lane closures
Weather Adverse weather conditions affecting travel time
Avoiding Tolls Took alternate route to avoid toll roads
Avoiding Freeway Used surface streets instead of highway/freeway
Alternate Location Started or ended at different location than system calculated
Note: You can add optional comments in the text field to provide additional context for the change.

8. Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Solutions

Issue: Overlap Warning Appears

  • Problem: 'Work time overlaps with travel to store. Would you like to recalculate travel to correct overlap?'
  • Solution: Click YES to let the system automatically recalculate travel time. The system will adjust the travel entry to eliminate overlap.

Issue: Forgot to Add Store to Home

  • Problem: Completed visit without adding Store to Home travel
  • Solution: View the completed visit using 'View Time' button. You can still add Store to Home travel from the completed visit screen using 'Add Store to Home' button. See Section 5 for details.

Issue: Accidentally Deleted Travel Entry

  • Problem: Deleted wrong travel entry during in-progress visit
  • Solution: Click 'Add additional details' and select 'Travel to Store' from dropdown. Click START to recreate the entry. See Section 4 for complete steps.

Issue: Mileage Seems Incorrect

  • Problem: System calculated mileage doesn't match actual travel
  • Solution: Contact your direct supervisor. Miles are automatically calculated and may require supervisor adjustment if GPS data is incorrect.

Issue: Can't Find Completed Visit

  • Problem: Need to edit travel for past visit but can't locate it
  • Solution: Navigate to Schedule screen and find the date of the visit. Click on the specific visit to expand details, then click 'View Time' button.

Issue: System Won't Accept Changes

  • Problem: Changes not saving or error message appears
  • Solution: Ensure you selected a Change Reason from the dropdown. All edits and deletions require a reason (see Section 7). If problem persists, contact your supervisor.
⚠ For any issues not listed here, please contact your direct supervisor immediately.
